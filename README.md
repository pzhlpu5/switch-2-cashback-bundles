If you have been sitting on the fence waiting for a discount, that is the news you were waiting for, and it is not the news you wanted.

## Why the price went up, in plain terms

The short version: memory chips got expensive, and consoles are full of them.

AI data centre buildouts pulled enormous capacity away from consumer electronics, and DRAM and NAND pricing climbed hard through 2025 and into 2026. Nintendo said the component and tariff pressure would cost it around ¥100 billion this financial year. Memory chips specifically are the driver — an LPDDR5X module isn't a glamorous part, but a shortage of it hits every console maker at once. Sony raised PS5 pricing in the US before Nintendo followed.

Nintendo also trimmed its own forecast: 19.86 million Switch 2 units sold in the financial year ending March 2026, with 16.5 million predicted for the following year, roughly 17% lower. Mario Kart World had moved 14.7 million copies by then, and Donkey Kong Bananza 4.52 million. Solid software numbers, softer hardware outlook.

None of that changes what you pay at checkout, but it does explain why no retailer is quietly eating the difference for you.

## The exclusion list is the part people miss

A cashback platform is only useful if the thing you want is eligible, and consoles sit on exclusion lists more often than you would expect.

ShopBack Australia's promotional terms for The Good Guys, for example, explicitly exclude the Nintendo Switch 2 alongside the PS5 and Xbox Series X 1TB from cashback — inside campaigns that otherwise advertise 10% or 15% headline rates. That is not a ShopBack-specific quirk; it is retail cashback generally. High-demand hardware with thin margins is exactly what merchants carve out.

So before you build a purchase plan around a cashback rate, do this:

- Open the specific merchant's ShopBack page, not the campaign banner.
- Read the Terms and Exclusions section, where bundled subscription codes, e-currency top-ups and gift cards usually appear.
- Check whether the exclusion names the product or only a category. "Consoles" and "Nintendo Switch 2" are different exclusion scopes.
- Confirm whether the rate applies to pre-orders and restocks on the SKU you want.

If the console itself is excluded, the purchase is still worth routing through cashback for everything else in the same order, provided the terms allow it.

## What actually stacks: games, controllers, storage

The console is the least cashback-friendly part of a Switch 2 setup. The accessories are the opposite.

Controllers and Joy-Con 2 pairs, carrying cases, screen protectors, charging docks, headsets, microSD Express cards and games all typically earn cashback on eligible order value at the major marketplaces. A Switch 2 with a 1TB microSD Express card, an extra controller and three games is a meaningfully larger order than the console alone.

Cashback also generally tracks on the qualifying order value after platform vouchers are applied, so a Lazada or Shopee voucher and cashback usually coexist. Coins redemption is the fiddly one — it can reduce the eligible order value depending on how the platform reports the order, so check before you redeem a balance you have been saving.

Here is the thing to weigh: don't buy accessories you don't need to chase a percentage. A 3% return on an unnecessary S$80 case is a S$77 loss.

## Questions people keep asking

**Is the Switch 2 region-locked?**
No, following the original Switch pattern. The hardware is identical across regions and cartridges are region-free. Japan-region cartridges can default to Japanese, and some publishers ship Japan SKUs without English, so verify per title if you are importing. Digital purchases follow your account region.

**Is GameChat free?**
It was open to everyone without a Nintendo Switch Online membership until 31 March 2026. After that, GameChat requires an NSO membership. Budget for the subscription on top of the console if you plan to use it.

**What is actually in the box?**
A 7.9-inch 1080p display, magnetic Joy-Con 2 with mouse-style control in compatible games, 4K output through the dock, a built-in noise-filtering microphone, and backwards compatibility with your existing Switch library for compatible titles.

**Is a Switch 2 Pro coming?**
Nintendo has not announced one. There has been no imminent refresh signalled, and given that the company just raised prices rather than refreshing the SKU, waiting for a mid-cycle upgrade is not a strategy with a date attached to it.

**Will prices come back down?**
Nothing Nintendo has said points that way. It framed the increases as reflecting conditions that "extend over the medium to long term," and it downgraded sales expectations at the same time. Treat the current price as the baseline, not a spike.

**Does buying from a grey-market seller save money?**
Sometimes on paper, and the warranty is the trade-off. Grey imports typically come with the shop's own warranty rather than regional coverage, and ShopBack only tracks purchases at its partner retailers — a Sim Lim or Carousell deal earns you nothing.
